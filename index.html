What it does

Log what you cook

Snap a photo, add a note and a rating, and it's saved with the date. Your own record, not a review site.

Save from anywhere

Paste a recipe, a link, or an Instagram post. Fork'd pulls out the ingredients and steps so you're not scrolling past a life story.

Cook what's in season

Suggestions based on what's actually growing where you are, so dinner starts with something worth cooking.

Cook together

Share a recipe with the people you cook with and keep the notes on it in one shared place.

Common questions

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This removes all your data.
  • A recipe import didn't work: some sites (e.g. Instagram) block automatic reading – paste the recipe text directly and Fork'd will still save it.
  • Photos: photos of your dishes are stored to your account and only shared with people you explicitly cook with.

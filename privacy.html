PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated October 31, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Hanna Bieri ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you download and use our mobile application fork'd.

Questions or concerns? Contact us at forkdcooking@gmail.com.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

What personal information do we process? Name, email address, username, password, cooking data, photos, authentication data, and records of which app features you use.

Do we process sensitive personal information? No.

Do we collect information from third parties? No.

How do we process your information? To provide and improve the app, keep it fair and safe from abuse, manage your account, and send administrative communications.

Do we share personal information? No. Supabase, Anthropic, OpenAI and Expo act as service processors on our behalf and do not receive your data as third parties.

How do we keep your information safe? Through Supabase's encryption at rest and in transit, and Row Level Security ensuring users can only access their own data.

How do you exercise your rights? In-app via Profile → Delete Account, or by contacting us at forkdcooking@gmail.com.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register or use the app:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Usernames
  • Passwords (or, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier, name and email address Apple provides, which may be a private relay address)
  • Cooking data (dish titles, notes, ingredients, cook dates)
  • Photos of dishes (taken via camera or selected from your library)
  • Contact or authentication data
  • Dietary preferences and food allergies
  • Country (used to tailor results)
  • Push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications
  • Usage data: which features you use and when (for example cooks logged, recipes saved or imported, and AI requests with their size and cost), and when you last used the app

We also request access to your device's camera and photo library solely to enable dish photography. We do not collect geolocation or contacts.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on your device. Usage data comes only from what our servers already receive when you use the app.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

  • To facilitate account creation and authentication.
  • To deliver the service. Including AI-powered ingredient detection from photos.
  • To send administrative information. Such as password reset emails.
  • To send push notifications. Such as when someone sends you a friend request or tags you in a cook. You can turn these off in your device settings.
  • To keep the service fair and safe. Such as daily limits on AI features, uploads and friend requests, and spotting abuse.
  • To understand how fork'd is used. Such as how many people use each feature and whether they come back, so we can improve the app. Only we look at this; it is not shared or used for advertising.

3. LEGAL BASES (EEA/UK)

We process your data on the basis of contractual necessity – to provide you with the fork'd service you signed up for.

We process usage data to keep fork'd fair and safe and to improve it, on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a reliable and affordable service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this at any time by emailing us. We will then stop, unless the processing is needed to prevent abuse.

4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION?

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

We use the following service processors who handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – database, file storage and authentication hosting (European Union, Ireland)
  • Anthropic – AI analysis of the photos, recipe text and chat messages you submit to fork'd's AI features (United States). Content is sent for processing under Anthropic's API terms and privacy policy.
  • OpenAI – generates the photo shown with a recipe suggested in the Ask chat, from that recipe's title and short description only (United States). Nothing about you is sent.
  • Expo – delivers push notifications to your device: your device's push token and the notification text, such as a friend's name (United States).

5. AI-BASED FEATURES

fork'd uses Anthropic's Claude AI to analyse photos of dishes and detect ingredients, to read recipe text or links you paste, and to suggest recipes in the Ask chat. When you use these features, what you submit (the photo, text or chat messages) is sent to Anthropic's API, together with your dietary preferences and country so results fit you (for Ask, also the titles and ingredients of your recent cooks). It is sent for this purpose only. We do not send your name or email with it.

When Ask suggests a full recipe, its photo is generated by OpenAI's image model from the recipe's title and short description. No photos, chat messages, preferences or account details of yours are sent to OpenAI.

Before you use an AI feature for the first time, fork'd asks for your permission. If you decline, nothing is sent and you can still add dishes and ingredients by hand. Your permission is remembered on your device and you can withdraw it by contacting us.

6. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Your account data, cooking data and photos are stored in the European Union (Ireland) by Supabase. What you submit to the AI features is processed in the United States by Anthropic and, for Ask recipe photos, OpenAI; push notifications are delivered through Expo in the United States. These transfers from the EEA/UK are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under our service agreements with these providers.

7. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time via Profile → Delete Account, which removes your account and all your data, including your photos. If you signed in with Apple, it also revokes fork'd's access to your Apple ID.

Usage data is kept for up to 12 months. When you delete your account, records of AI use are kept only as anonymous totals, with nothing that links them to you.

8. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

We use Supabase's encrypted infrastructure and Row Level Security policies to ensure your data is accessible only to you, apart from the dishes and recipes you choose to share with friends or via a link. Photos can be opened by anyone who has their link. No electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

No. fork'd is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

To exercise these rights, use the in-app delete account feature or contact us at forkdcooking@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. DO-NOT-TRACK

We do not respond to Do-Not-Track signals as we do not track users across third-party websites.

12. US STATE PRIVACY RIGHTS

16. SHARING WITH FRIENDS

fork'd lets you connect with friends and share cooks and recipes with them, or via a link. Only the dishes and recipes you choose to tag or share are visible to the people you share them with. You can remove a friend at any time from Profile or Friends.

17. ACCEPTABLE USE, REPORTING AND BLOCKING

By using fork'd you agree not to post or share content that is unlawful, abusive, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit or otherwise objectionable, or that infringes someone else's rights. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

You can report a person (long-press a friend in Profile or Friends) or a shared recipe (the flag button on the shared recipe) from within the app, and you can block a person so they can no longer connect with you. We review reports and will remove content and suspend or remove accounts that break these rules, normally within 48 hours of a report. You can also email us at forkdcooking@gmail.com.
